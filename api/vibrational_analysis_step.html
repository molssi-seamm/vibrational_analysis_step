vibrational_analysis_step.fermi module#

The variational treatment of Fermi resonances in generalized VPT2 (GVPT2).

The deperturbed VPT2 energies of the states in a resonance – a fundamental and the overtone or combination band it is resonant with – are the diagonal of a small effective Hamiltonian, and the cubic force constant that couples them its off-diagonal elements. States joined by any chain of resonances form one polyad, whose Hamiltonian is diagonalized; each state takes the eigenvalue of the eigenvector it dominates.

Adapted from pyVPT2’s fermi_solver.py (Copyright 2021-2024 Philip Nelson, BSD-3-Clause; see THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.rst).

NOISE = {'quartic': 5.0}#

The spreads between equivalent finite-difference routes (cm^-1) above which the force constants are too noisy to trust to a few cm^-1. Only the quartic spread: it grows with noise as 1/step^2 (Hessians) or 1/step^3 (gradients), while the cubic spread at the default steps is mostly the higher-order (truncation) error, harmless (propane, 0.20: cubic 6.6, quartic 3.4 cm^-1, ZPE within 0.2 cm^-1 of Gaussian’s).

vibrational_analysis_step.vpt2 module#

Second-order vibrational perturbation theory (VPT2), independent of any code.

The anharmonic frequencies and zero-point energy of a molecule at a minimum, from

  1. its Cartesian Hessian there (Harmonic: the normal modes and harmonic frequencies, rotational constants and Coriolis coupling constants);

  2. Hessians, or gradients, at geometries displaced along the normal modes (displacements()), whatever code or model computes them;

  3. the cubic and semi-diagonal quartic force constants by finite differences (force_constants_from_hessians(), force_constants_from_gradients());

  4. VPT2 with Fermi resonances deperturbed, optionally treated variationally (GVPT2) (vpt2()).

Units: atomic units in, except masses in amu; the force constants and all results in cm^-1, in dimensionless (reduced) normal coordinates q_i = sqrt(omega_i) Q_i.

Only molecules without degenerate modes (asymmetric tops) are handled: the formulas for degenerate modes, needed by linear molecules and symmetric and spherical tops, are not yet in, so Harmonic refuses those molecules.

The VPT2 formulas, the detection of Fermi resonances and the finite-difference assembly are adapted from pyVPT2 (Copyright 2021-2024 Philip Nelson, BSD-3-Clause; see THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.rst).

vibrational_analysis_step.vpt2.mass_weighted_steps(harmonic, size=0.025)[source]#

Steps of the same size in mass-weighted Cartesian coordinates for every mode (Gaussian’s convention, 0.025 Å amu^1/2), as dimensionless steps: q_i = Q sqrt(omega_i), so stiff modes get larger dimensionless steps and floppy ones smaller. Against Gaussian 09 (2026-11-05) a uniform 0.05 was too small for C-H stretches: Hessian noise dominated the quartic constants (propane ZPE 44 cm^-1 off; 0.2 cm^-1 at a step like Gaussian’s).
