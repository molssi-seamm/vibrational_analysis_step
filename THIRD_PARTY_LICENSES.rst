pyVPT2
======

Parts of ``vibrational_analysis_step/vpt2.py`` and ``vibrational_analysis_step/fermi.py`` --
the VPT2 formulas for the anharmonicity constants and the zero-point energy, the detection of
Fermi resonances, the finite-difference assembly of the cubic and quartic force constants, and
the solver for the resonance polyads -- are adapted from pyVPT2
(https://github.com/philipmnel/pyvpt2, commit ab6b5b8, 2026-10-19), which is distributed under
